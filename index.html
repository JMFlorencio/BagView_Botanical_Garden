About BagView

BagView

Version 2.0

BagView is an AR-powered eco-tourism web app that lets visitors point their device at markers around the garden to instantly unlock interactive stories, photos, and facts about each station. Built to make exploring our green spaces more engaging, it turns every walkway into a self-guided, augmented reality tour, making learning about local plants and heritage sites more immersive without needing a printed guide or a tour staff on hand.
